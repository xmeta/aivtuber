= ADR-0012: Bounded functional regulation layer evaluated against the affect baseline
:status: Proposed
:date: 2026-11-25

== Context

Issue #486 (`priority:P2`, `status:blocked`, `status:design-ready`, Stage 4, tracked by #94) asks to compare a small, bounded regulation layer against the existing #86 affect baseline to determine whether tracking accumulated load and recovery improves emotional continuity and coordinated non-verbal presence more than valence/arousal decay alone.

This ADR records a proposed comparison design and architecture boundary only. Implementation remains blocked until #86 exposes a measured deterministic affect baseline and #72 defines compatible persona bounds. It authorizes no production code, changes no wire schemas, adds no dependencies, and leaves all v0.3 defaults unchanged.

Inputs this ADR builds on, present on `main` at `e40c722`:

* `crates/generative/src/affect.rs`: per-reply bounded `AffectState` (`AffectClass`, `AffectStrength`, `AffectConfig`, anti-repetition hold/relaxation) and `crates/app/src/affect_judge.rs` (optional System One affect class hint).
* #86 (session-scoped bounded affect/energy continuity with decay and hysteresis — parent baseline to compare against).
* #72 (typed persona contract bounds), #84 (`PerformanceIntent`, ADR-0006), #107 (continuous listening-performance prior), #87 (`InitiativeDirector`, advisory consumer only), #191 (`RoleBlend`, explicitly separate), #58 (benchmark/telemetry), and #59 (blind pairwise review).

These regulation variables are *functional control variables*, not biological measurements, literal hormone chemistry, or claims of subjective emotion.

== Hard constraints

* *Single affect ownership and scheduler authority*: Do not create a competing `AffectState` or scheduler. The regulation state sits alongside #86's logical clock, validated event impulse boundary, bounds, and replay metadata.
* *Strict non-authority and non-tampering* (`A-CTRL-001`): Untrusted chat and model outputs can never directly set regulation variables or bypass community moderation, floor control, scheduler priorities, operator stop/mute, safety gates, or task verification.
* *Read-only advisory projection only*: Regulation state projects a compact read-only summary to #84 `PerformanceIntent` and #107 listening prior (once available), and may be read by #87 as advisory input only. No direct actuator writes, no extra LLM calls, no new sidecar, and no frame-loop inference.
* *Session-bounded, opt-in, and non-profiling*: Off by default (`default = off`); reset cleanly on session end or operator reset. No durable viewer emotional profiles, no external self-preservation objectives, and no forced rest or autonomous shutdown.
* *Deterministic logical-clock replay*: Pure deterministic update function over bounded inputs and monotonic logical time; resistant to clock anomalies (regression/large jumps) and chat bursts/spam.
* *At most two additional independent state dimensions*: Dimensions must correspond to concrete observed failure cases (abrupt reset, over-persistence, flat exhaustion after sustained intensity), not metaphorical hormone names.

== Assumptions

Existing invariants referenced:

* `A-CTRL-001` (Invariant): untrusted content and model output never hold runtime authority.
* `A-MEM-001` (Invariant): durable memory writes require non-serializable `MemoryWritePermit`.

Proposed new assumptions (to be added to link:../assumptions.adoc[Assumption Register] only if accepted):

* *A-REG-001* (*Needs validation*): Adding at most two bounded functional dimensions (`strain` and `recovery`) reduces abrupt emotional resets and unnatural over-persistence after sustained high-intensity gameplay or bursty chat compared to valence/arousal decay alone. *Evidence:* unverified hypothesis. *Revisit when:* #59 blind comparison shows no meaningful improvement over #86 baseline or higher instability/cost.
* *A-REG-002* (*Needs validation*): Fixed-point/clamped deterministic decay with per-window impulse caps prevents chat spam or repetitive bursts from saturating `strain` into permanent flatness. *Evidence:* unverified; must be tested on synthetic burst/spam fixtures before any live enablement.

== Alternatives considered

. *Status quo (#86 valence/arousal decay baseline)*: simplest and lowest complexity. Retained as the mandatory baseline; if the candidate fails to show a clear blind-review win on held-out scenarios, stay with status quo and reject the added state.
. *Literal neurochemical / hormone simulation (e.g., dopamine/cortisol/serotonin curves)*: rejected. Adds uncalibrated coupled parameters, invites anthropomorphic claims, and obscures failure attribution without improving deterministic control.
. *LLM-driven mood narration or external emotion sidecar*: rejected. Adds latency, non-determinism, VRAM/cost overhead, and prompt-injection exposure; violates #486's zero-extra-LLM constraint.
. *Two-dimension bounded functional regulation (`strain`, `recovery`) as a pure deterministic update function (proposed)*: minimal extra state, directly tied to concrete continuity failures, zero extra LLM calls, and cleanly testable off vs on against identical #86 streams.

== Evidence

* *Repository executable evidence*:
  - `crates/generative/src/affect.rs` proves bounded per-reply `AffectState` and `AffectConfig` preset clamping (`AFFECT_INTENSITY_CAP`, `MAX_AVAILABLE_PRESETS`).
  - `crates/app/src/affect_judge.rs` proves affect hints remain advisory and fall back cleanly to deterministic rules.
* *Unverified expectation (explicitly not yet measured)*:
  - No measured #86 session-level affect baseline or #72 persona-bound coupling exists yet.
  - All decay constants, saturation caps, and hysteresis bands below are analytical starting points for synthetic offline comparison, not calibrated production parameters.

== Decision

=== 1. Two bounded functional dimensions (not hormones)

Select exactly two independent clamped state variables in `[0.0, 1.0]` (or fixed-scale milli-units `0..=1000` for bit-exact deterministic replay):

* *`strain`* (`0..=1000`, baseline `0`): accumulated short-to-medium-term interaction/gameplay intensity load. Targets *abrupt reset* (prevents snapping instantly from peak excitement to flat neutral on a single quiet tick) and *burst/spam resistance* (impulse contribution per logical window is strictly capped).
* *`recovery`* (`0..=1000`, baseline `1000`): remaining expressiveness/pacing headroom over a long stream session. Targets *over-persistence* (gently dampens repeated peak arousal spikes after sustained high-load stretches and recovers smoothly during quiet stretches without ever forcing rest or silence).

=== 2. Pure deterministic update contract

[source,text]
----
RegulationInput (validated by #86 / normalizer, never raw untrusted chat):
  now_ms: u64                     // Monotonic logical clock (clamped on regression; dt capped on large jumps)
  impulse_magnitude: u16          // 0..=1000, derived from validated game/scene/affect events
  impulse_source: ImpulseSource   // TrustedGameEvent | ValidatedAffectReading | QuietTick | OperatorReset
  persona_bounds: PersonaRegBounds // From #72 (max_strain_gain, recovery_rate_bounds)

update(state, input) -> (next_state, RegulationSummary, reason_code):
  1. If OperatorReset: return (RegulationState::baseline(), summary, "operator_reset").
  2. Clamp dt = min(now_ms.saturating_sub(state.last_ms), MAX_STEP_MS).
  3. Cap impulse contribution per sliding logical window so burst/spam cannot peg `strain`.
  4. Apply bounded exponential/piecewise-linear decay on `strain` and recovery on `recovery`.
  5. Apply hysteresis band before flipping discrete summary tier (Calm | Engaged | HighLoad | CoolingDown).
----

=== 3. Read-only projection boundaries

`RegulationSummary` is strictly read-only advisory data:

* *To #84 (`PerformanceIntent`)*: bounded intensity/tempo modulation hint (never overrides safety, operator mute/stop, or pack preset caps).
* *To #107 (listening prior)*: subtle posture/settle pacing hint during quiet recovery.
* *To #87 (`InitiativeDirector`)*: optional advisory input to utility scoring (ADR-0011); never triggers speech or bypasses suppression gates.
* *Separate from #191 (`RoleBlend`)*: task-vs-entertainer role balance remains owned by #191.

=== 4. Cheapest falsifier & go/no-go gate (before implementation)

Once #86 exposes a measured affect baseline and #72 defines compatible persona bounds:

1. Build deterministic timestamped synthetic scenarios covering all 7 regimes required by #486:
   - game wins and losses;
   - sudden surprise spike;
   - repetitive / spammy chat burst (verifying spam resistance);
   - quiet recovery stretch;
   - long stream duration (testing boundedness and memory plateau);
   - operator reset and clock anomalies (regression / large gap);
   - degraded-provider regime (zero extra LLM calls).
2. Replay identical event streams, persona, seed, budget, and logical timestamps with `regulation = off` (#86 baseline) vs `regulation = on`, logging reason codes and config/version.
3. Run #58 performance/overhead checks (update cost, p95 overhead, memory plateau, zero extra LLM calls) and #59 blind comparison (`abrupt reset`, `over-persistence`, `affect/voice/motion coherence`, `initiative disruption`, `both-unacceptable`).
4. *Go / no-go decision*: reject the added regulation state if held-out blind comparison shows no meaningful improvement over #86 baseline, or if instability, initiative disruption, or runtime cost rises.

== Consequences

=== Positive

* Addresses concrete multi-minute emotional continuity failures (abrupt reset, over-persistence) with at most two interpretable, bounded control variables.
* Zero extra LLM calls, zero external dependencies, zero durable viewer profiling, and bit-exact logical-clock replay.
* Cleanly falsifiable off-vs-on against identical #86 streams.

=== Costs / trade-offs

* Adds two state variables, hysteresis parameters, and persona-bound coupling (#72) that must be calibrated and maintained.
* If #86's own valence/arousal decay already solves continuity well enough, this layer would be unnecessary complexity — hence the explicit reject gate.

=== Known limitations

* Cannot be calibrated or promoted until #86 baseline and #72 persona bounds exist.
* Synthetic scenarios validate boundedness, spam resistance, and mechanics, not live audience perception; human-rated #59 blind review remains mandatory before any promotion.

== Staging and rollback

1. *Stage 0 (this ADR)*: design and synthetic falsifier specification only; zero runtime change.
2. *Stage 1 (offline replay comparison)*: run candidate pure update function against #86 baseline on synthetic scenarios; document go/no-go decision in #486.
3. *Stage 2 (opt-in shadow/live flag, `default = off`)*: only if Stage 1 passes blind #59 and #58 guardrails; rollback is toggling the flag off (preserves exact #86 baseline).
4. *Stage 3 (promotion)*: requires separate review after representative live evidence.

== Revisit conditions

* #86 baseline alone passes #59 continuity evaluations without abrupt reset or over-persistence failures (reject #486).
* Synthetic burst/spam or clock-anomaly tests reveal oscillation or runaway saturation despite impulse caps.
* #72 persona bounds or #84 `PerformanceIntent` modulation contract changes materially.
